# 07 – Staying Current: News Sources & Routine

*Curated October 2026.*

## Overview

You can't read everything. The goal is a **small, high-signal set of sources** plus a **routine**, so that you hear about the big things within a day or two and gradually build depth. Pick about 2 daily sources, 3–4 weekly newsletters and 1–2 podcasts. Don't subscribe to all of these.

## Recommended starter set (if you only do this)

1.  **Daily, 10 min:** skim the [Hacker News front page](https://news.ycombinator.com/) *or* [TLDR](https://tldr.tech/) (email).
2.  **Weekly:** [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/) (free tier), [Simon Willison's weblog](https://simonwillison.net/) for AI, and [tl;dr sec](https://tldrsec.com/) for security.
3.  **Podcast for walks/commutes:** [Hard Fork](https://www.nytimes.com/column/hard-fork) (tech news, accessible) or [Acquired](https://www.acquired.fm/) (company histories).

## Aggregators (daily)

  - [**Hacker News**](https://news.ycombinator.com/): what engineers are talking about. The comments are often better than the articles, but be skeptical of the confident contrarians.
  - [**Lobsters**](https://lobste.rs/): smaller and more technical than HN, with less business news.
  - [**Techmeme**](https://www.techmeme.com/): a one-page summary of the day's tech business news, with links to every outlet's coverage.
  - [**TLDR**](https://tldr.tech/): free daily email digests. There are specific editions for AI, web dev, infosec and more; pick 1–2.

## Newsletters and blogs (weekly)

### General engineering

  - [**The Pragmatic Engineer – Gergely Orosz**](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/): inside Big Tech and startups, engineering culture and the job market.
  - [**ByteByteGo – Alex Xu**](https://blog.bytebytego.com/): system design with diagrams.

### AI

  - [**Simon Willison**](https://simonwillison.net/): hands-on and skeptical. The best single source for "what actually changed in AI this week".
  - [**Latent Space**](https://www.latent.space/): the AI engineer's newsletter and podcast. Coined "AI engineer".
  - [**Import AI – Jack Clark**](https://importai.substack.com/): weekly research roundup plus policy (Clark co-founded Anthropic).
  - [**The Batch – DeepLearning.AI**](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/): Andrew Ng's accessible weekly AI news.
  - [**Interconnects – Nathan Lambert**](https://www.interconnects.ai/): deeper takes on model training, open models and RL.

### Security

  - [**tl;dr sec**](https://tldrsec.com/): the best curated weekly security newsletter.
  - [**Krebs on Security**](https://krebsonsecurity.com/): investigative cybercrime reporting.
  - [**Risky Business**](https://risky.biz/): the leading weekly security news podcast.

### Frontend

  - [**Bytes**](https://bytes.dev/): a funny weekly JS ecosystem newsletter.

### Business and strategy

  - [**Stratechery – Ben Thompson**](https://stratechery.com/): why tech companies make the moves they do. The free weekly article is worth reading.

### Mainstream tech journalism (for big stories)

  - [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/), [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/) (more technical), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/) (startups and funding), [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/) (investigative), and [Wired](https://www.wired.com/).

## Podcasts and YouTube

  - [**Hard Fork (NYT)**](https://www.nytimes.com/column/hard-fork): weekly tech news with a lot of AI.
  - [**Acquired**](https://www.acquired.fm/): company deep dives.
  - [**Darknet Diaries**](https://darknetdiaries.com/): hacking stories.
  - [**Latent Space podcast**](https://www.latent.space/podcast): interviews with AI builders.
  - [**Fireship (YouTube)**](https://www.youtube.com/@Fireship): "X in 100 seconds" explainers and fast, meme-heavy news. Excellent for learning what a technology *is* quickly.
  - [**ByteByteGo (YouTube)**](https://www.youtube.com/@ByteByteGo): animated system design.
  - [**Andrej Karpathy (YouTube)**](https://www.youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy): occasional but definitive AI explainers.
  - [**Computerphile (YouTube)**](https://www.youtube.com/@Computerphile): university-style CS explainers from the University of Nottingham.

## Big threads to follow right now (as of fall 2026)

Use these as "folders" in your head when reading news:

1.  **AI agents and coding agents**: autonomous tools doing real work, MCP adoption, and how much code is now AI-written.
2.  **AI infrastructure and energy**: massive data center spending, nuclear power deals, water use, and Nvidia vs. AMD vs. custom chips (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium).
3.  **Open-weight models and China**: DeepSeek, Qwen and others vs. US labs, plus chip export controls.
4.  **AI regulation and policy**: the EU AI Act rollout, US federal vs. state rules, FTC scrutiny of AI companies, and restrictions on frontier models with strong cyber capabilities.
5.  **Software supply-chain attacks**: compromised packages, extensions and developer machines.
6.  **Big Tech restructuring**: layoffs and reorgs framed as "investing in AI", and the effect on the engineering job market.

## Routine

  - **Daily (10–15 min):** HN front page or TLDR. Open at most 2 articles. For any term you don't recognize, look it up in a Fireship "in 100 seconds" video or the guides in this folder.
  - **Weekly (1 hr):** read 2 newsletter issues in full and work through one item from a topic guide (01–05).
  - **Monthly:** write a 5-bullet "what changed this month" note to yourself. Explaining it is what makes it stick.
  - **Tip:** use an RSS reader such as [Feedly](https://feedly.com/) or [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/) to put blogs in one place instead of your email inbox.
